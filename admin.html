    Free up a username

    For a player who lost their username, for example because their browser data was cleared before they set a password, or who forgot their password. This removes the username, its login and its backed-up save, so they can create it again as a new account. Then send them their silver and items again as a gift.

    Leaderboard

    The top 3 in every game mode, as players see them. Remove a record that looks fake. The next player in line moves up, and the spot opens for the next real score.

    Remove spam accounts

    Finds every player whose username starts with the word tester, then removes their username, player entry, save, requests and feedback. Tap once to see how many it found, then tap again to delete them.
